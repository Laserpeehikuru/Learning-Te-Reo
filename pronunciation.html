Let's Learn To Pronounce Māori!

Learning the sounds of Te Reo Māori can help us speak more confidently. Start by learning the five vowels, then practise the A Ha Ka Ma pattern.

NGĀ PUARE

The Five Vowels

Learn the five vowel sounds used in Te Reo Māori.
A E I O U

Practise saying each vowel out loud.

TE RAUPAPA A HA KA MA

A Ha Ka Ma

Practise the Māori sounds by following each vowel row.
A Ha Ka Ma Na Pa Ra Ta Wa Nga Wha
E He Ke Me Ne Pe Re Te We Nge Whe
I Hi Ki Mi Ni Pi Ri Ti Wi Ngi Whi
O Ho Ko Mo No Po Ro To Wo Ngo Who
U Hu Ku Mu Nu Pu Ru Tu Wu Ngu Whu
