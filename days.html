Days of the Week

Ngā Rā o te Wiki

Learn the Māori names for each day of the week and practise using them in everyday life.

📅

NGĀ RĀ

Learn The Days

A good way to remember the days of the week is to practise saying them as you go through your normal week. Try using the Māori names whenever you can!
