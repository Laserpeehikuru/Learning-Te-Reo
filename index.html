Appreciating Culture

Learning about our Māori culture helps us know who we are and where we come from. By learning te reo Māori, tikanga, whakapapa, and the stories of our tūpuna, we keep our culture strong and pass it on to the next generation. It can also help us feel proud, connected, and confident in who we are. Every word we learn and every tradition we share helps keep our culture alive!

Ngā akoranga me te tupuranga

→

Learning and Growth

Learning Te Reo = Training Your Brain! Learning te reo Māori is good for more than just learning a language — it gives your brain a workout! Speaking two languages can help you **focus, remember things, solve problems, and make decisions. Learning a language can also be easier when you're younger because your brain is still developing and is good at picking up new sounds and words. But don't worry — **it's never too late to learn!** Anyone can learn te reo Māori with practice, patience, and effort. Every new word you learn is another step towards growing your **language skills and your connection to te ao Māori.

→

Strengthen Identity

Know Your Culture, Know Yourself! Your culture is a big part of who you are and where you come from. Learning about your whānau, whakapapa, tūpuna, and traditions can help you feel connected and proud of who you are. Staying connected to your culture can also help you feel confident, supported, and like you belong — wherever you go. So remember: use your culture, dont lose your culture!

Connecting With Family

Culture Brings Us Together! Culture is what makes a group of people unique and connected. It includes things like language, traditions, stories, beliefs, music, and art. Culture helps us remember where we come from and gives us things to share with others. It brings people together through the things we have in common, while also making every culture special in its own way. Our culture connects us to our past, our people, and our future!
