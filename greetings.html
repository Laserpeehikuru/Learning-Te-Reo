AKO 01 • LESSON ONE

Greetings

Ngā Mihi

Learn some common Te Reo Māori greetings that you can use when talking with others.

👋

HAERE MAI

Let's Get Started!

Greetings are an important part of communication. Learning how to greet people in Te Reo Māori is a great first step towards using the language in everyday life.

NGĀ KUPU

Common Greetings

Here are some useful greetings to get you started.
01
👋

Kia ora

Hello / Hi

A common greeting used when saying hello to someone.

02
😊

Tēnā koe

Hello to one person

A respectful way to greet one person.

03
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Tēnā kōrua

Hello to two people

Used when greeting two people.

04
👥

Tēnā koutou

Hello to three or more people

Used when greeting a group of three or more people.

05
🌿

Kia ora koutou

Hello everyone

A friendly greeting used when speaking to a group of people.

06
🏡

Nau mai

Welcome

Used to welcome someone or invite them to come in.

KŌRERO

Try It In A Conversation

Now let's put some of the words you've learned into a simple conversation.

🧑

👩
Person B
